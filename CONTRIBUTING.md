# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in contributing to this repository.

## How to Contribute

We welcome:
- High-level documentation about iOS security architecture
- Public-source references and notes relevant to lawful security research
- Clarifications to existing documents and structured notes

Please avoid:
- Jailbreak or exploit development
- Bypassing or weakening device protections
- Publishing unauthorized access instructions or payloads

## Pull Requests

Before submitting a pull request, please:
1. Check that your change matches the repository’s lawful research scope.
2. Ensure your material is accurate and appropriately cited.
3. Keep contributions focused, clear, and respectful.

## Reporting Concerns

If you believe a contribution violates the repository’s goals or safety policies, please open an issue or contact the maintainers.
